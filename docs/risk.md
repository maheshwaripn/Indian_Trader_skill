# Educational purpose and user responsibility

This project is educational and research software. It is not personalised investment advice, an assurance of profit, a broker service or a regulatory approval. You choose strategies, data, capital, limits and deployment decisions and bear the resulting financial risk. Past or simulated performance does not predict future returns. Losses can exceed planned stop risk because of gaps, illiquidity, rejected orders, outages and other failures.

The software is provided under the repository licence without warranty. This notice does not eliminate applicable legal responsibilities or override broker terms. Obtain qualified advice where the proposed activity requires it.

## Freedom with transparent consequences

Research risk parameters are user-configurable. The program does not impose a universal investment preference. It rejects invalid numerical inputs, impossible unsupported operations, duplicate fills and inconsistent data because these are correctness failures. Unsupported leverage, shorts and derivatives are implementation limits, not an assertion that users may never research them elsewhere.

An AI provider retains its own permissions and policies. The repo cannot remove them. Direct Python operation provides independence from model availability without bypassing access controls, regulations or provider rules.

## Evidence labels

- `SYNTHETIC_DEMO_NOT_MARKET_DATA`: generated prices for demonstrating software.
- `HISTORICAL_SIMULATION`: supplied history with explicit model assumptions.
- `MANUAL_PAPER_LEDGER`: simulated fills entered by the user; no broker execution.
- `REQUIRES_RECONCILIATION`: inconsistent constituent history requiring investigation.

No live-performance label is generated by v0.1. Reports must not be marketed as verified returns. Keep uncertainty visible, including unavailable benchmark, corporate-action and historical-universe evidence.
